What is Zoho CRM?

Zoho CRM is a customer relationship management application used to manage leads, contacts, accounts, deals, activities, sales processes, and customer communications.

Zoho CRM can be customized to match a company's business processes using fields, layouts, workflows, blueprints, validation rules, custom functions, reports, dashboards, and integrations.

What is Workflow Automation?

Workflow automation uses predefined rules to automatically perform actions when a specific event occurs. For example:

  • Send an email when a new lead is created.
  • Update a field when a deal reaches a specific stage.
  • Create a task when a record meets certain conditions.
  • Send information to another application through an API.
